# Source rights and public boundary

Source: U.S. Energy Information Administration, EIA-923 final historical annual 2018–2024 archives, captured 29 October 2026 with exact archive/workbook hashes. Current EIA copyrights/reuse guidance permits attributed federal-data reuse: https://www.eia.gov/about/copyrights_reuse.php . No EIA endorsement is implied.

The v2 public projection contains only year/worksheet-row locators, infrastructure plant IDs, state/CHP/prime-mover/fuel/nuclear-unit labels, annual electricity-fuel MMBtu and net-generation MWh raw tokens/types, and selected-field hashes. These are selected federal factual records, not plant/operator/contact names, coordinates, monetary costs, logos or artwork. The original complete archives/workbooks and guides remain private.

V2 whole-plant quarantine is a project transformation fixed before decomposition. Ambiguous rows remain outside the numerical cohort; their quantities are neither added into a coverage total nor assigned a speculative partition. Source hashes, identity/classification reasons and row locators document exclusions. The narrower reporting cohort is not all national generation or all non-CHP plants. The failed original source gate remains unchanged.

MIT covers the project's code. The EIA reuse basis applies to its attributed factual data. Separate formal-publication projects, including Azure optimization, credentials and excluded private material remain outside this public package.
