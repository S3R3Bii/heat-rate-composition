# Separating reported heat-rate change from generation-share change

## Decision and fixed scope

How much of a change in selected fleet heat rate comes from changes in reported cell fuel/generation ratios versus changes in generation shares and cohort composition? Use EIA-923 annual final historical source workbooks for 2018-2024. This is a bounded historical design, not the latest current-year assessment or a real-time forecast. Exact annual fields and workbook aliases must be mapped from headers before inspecting data values. Source HEAD readiness and official documentation have been checked; no fuel/generation values have been read before this protocol.

Select explicit non-CHP plants in the 50 US states/DC and coal fuels ANT, BIT, LIG, SUB, RC, WC or natural gas NG. Exclude unknown CHP status and territories, retaining exclusion counts. Analyze coal, natural gas and pooled selected fuel groups separately. A fuel group at a plant is not a whole multi-fuel plant's engineering efficiency. No claim about renewables or the complete national fleet follows. Use published annual electricity fuel consumption in MMBtu and net generation in MWh, not receipts, total fuel for useful heat, gross generation or electricity sold. No private fuel costs, contacts, operator names, plant names or precise coordinates will be published.

Aggregate source components before positivity checks. Primary cells are plant/fuel-group/year, summing all reported prime movers. Sensitivity cells are plant/technology-group/fuel-group/year. Combine CA, CT and CS into one CC group before eligibility to avoid treating fuel-free combined-cycle steam generation as a stand-alone fuel-efficiency observation. Other documented prime mover codes remain distinct. Unknown codes require a source clarification before analysis. Preserve source fields, raw numeric XML tokens, worksheet and row locators. Reject duplicate source plant/prime-mover/fuel keys unless a documented additional source dimension is prospectively incorporated before analysis. Plant-year state and CHP status must be internally consistent; conflicting keys fail the quality gate. An aggregate cell with missing/malformed fuel or generation components is unavailable; no zero imputation. Nonpositive aggregate fuel or net generation is ineligible, with counts and signed excluded totals retained. Negative source generation components are allowed in the pre-eligibility sum; they must not silently disappear.

## Fixed comparisons and arithmetic

For each of the six adjacent-year pairs, compare three fuel fleets, two aggregation grains and two matched-cohort definitions: pair-common eligible cells, and cells eligible in all seven years. Thus 72 cases (36 development/36 held). The 2018-2019, 2019-2020 and 2020-2021 pairs are development; 2021-2022, 2022-2023 and 2023-2024 are held analytical summaries. The boundary year is shared; all source inputs/quality are visible without blinding. There is no predictive-generalization test. Freeze all source/protocol/analysis dependencies after fixtures/development checks and before computing held decomposition outcomes. No choice of outcome, cohort, period, label or parameter follows held inspection. The balanced cohort deliberately uses all seven years and is a retrospective sensitivity, not information available to a historical decision maker.

Within a matched cohort M, h_it=f_it/g_it, s_it=g_it/sum_M(g_it), and H_Mt=sum_M(f_it)/sum_M(g_it). Define the symmetric two-factor decomposition:

Within = sum_M[(s_i0+s_i1)(h_i1-h_i0)/2].
Mix = sum_M[(h_i0+h_i1)(s_i1-s_i0)/2].

Within + Mix = H_M1-H_M0. Retain both factor-order paths as well: within at initial shares plus mix at final ratios, and within at final shares plus mix at initial ratios. The symmetric result is their arithmetic average, not a fitted attribution. The full eligible fleet bridge is H_1-H_0 = Within + Mix + [(H_1-H_M1)-(H_0-H_M0)]. Label the final bracket **outside-cohort composition remainder**. It includes eligibility/reporting and matching changes and is not a count or causal effect of physical plant entry/retirement. Do not fill undefined unmatched h values with zero. If M or a denominator is empty/nonpositive, retain NOT_ESTIMABLE and the reason. Report matched counts and generation coverage at both endpoints.

Aggregate source decimals exactly. Use Decimal precision 60 for rate/attribution arithmetic, with independent Decimal precision 80 and an absolute 1e-45 MMBtu/MWh tolerance fixed before values. Exact categorical/count/source-key equality remains mandatory. This is a deterministic numerical-accuracy budget, not a measurement-precision or empirical optimization claim. Tiny rational fixtures independently verify both factor paths, symmetric identity and membership bridge. Floating conversion is restricted to displays/plots. Retain observation-level matched cell contributions and every case, including negative components and failures. No hyperparameter search or ML is needed.

## Sensitivity and independent checks

For each of the 18 primary adjacent-year/fleet cases, delete each matched plant in turn, removing all its matched fuel cells. Retain every resulting matched within/mix/change, or NOT_ESTIMABLE. This finite leave-one-plant influence range is not a confidence interval or future uncertainty bound. Efficient algebra may subtract plant totals and cross-ratio sums; independent checks must reconstruct those quantities separately and compare selected direct deletions. Do not tune an outlier filter or discard an influential plant. No p-values, causal improvement, counterfactual engineering efficiency, savings or exhaustive novelty claims.

Independent source verification must compare the original worksheet schema, keys, raw XML numeric tokens and independent workbook-reader values with explicit floating conversion bounds, then reconcile exact grouped fuel/generation values using SQL/decimal arithmetic. An independent result checker cannot import production aggregation/decomposition helpers. Check complete case lattice, source exclusions, matched/balanced membership, both factor paths, every per-cell contribution, all bridges and influence exports. Isolated replay must reproduce deterministic outputs; original per-case runtime records are retained separately from variable replay timings.
